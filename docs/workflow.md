# PE/VC research workflow

## Stage 1: Industry understanding

**Input:** an industry or product category, geography, time horizon, and investment context.

**Skill:** `pevc-industry-chain-mapper`

**Output:** structured industry data and an interactive value-chain map.

**Quality gate:** the map reflects actual product, service, and demand flows; each node has relevant companies, evidence, metrics, investment observations, and sources.

## Stage 2: Company diligence

**Input:** the target company, transaction stage, available company materials, public sources, financial information, and questions raised by the industry map.

**Skill:** `pevc-company-diligence`

**Output:** company diligence report, evidence table, prioritized questions, red flags, and next actions.

**Quality gate:** important commercial and financial claims have an evidence label; customer status and metric definitions are reconciled; the report explains what could change the view.

## Stage 3: Investment decision

**Input:** industry work, company diligence, transaction terms, capitalization, valuation or return model, and residual risks.

**Skill:** `pevc-ic-memo`

**Output:** screening, preliminary, final, or update IC memo.

**Quality gate:** the requested decision, recommendation, assumptions, scenarios, risks, unresolved items, and approval conditions are visible and internally consistent.

## Handoff rules

- Industry facts do not automatically prove company access or execution.
- Company quality does not automatically justify the proposed price or structure.
- A management target remains a claim until independently supported.
- Every handoff should preserve source references, dates, definitions, calculation assumptions, and evidence status.
- When conclusions change, record the new evidence and the prior conclusion rather than overwriting history silently.

## Evidence handling across all stages

- Use only materials the user or organization is authorized to process.
- Keep confidential source files and generated deal work products outside the repository.
- Preserve claim-level provenance without exposing access tokens, private links, personal data, or internal absolute file paths.
- Before sharing an output externally, verify that its citations are accessible to the intended audience and that confidential evidence is described without being reproduced.
- Link to third-party methods where practical. Do not copy proprietary Skill or Workflow text without permission and license compatibility.
