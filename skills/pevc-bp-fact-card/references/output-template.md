## 2. Product, customer, and value

Explain what the product actually does, where it sits in the customer's workflow, what it replaces, required complements, purchase reason, implementation, evidence of use, and current maturity. Separate marketing language from observable facts.

## 3. Industry and chain position

| Input | Transformation | Output | Immediate customer | Payer | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

List market, competition, policy, and ranking claims that require external verification.

## 4. Business model and operating progress

Describe pricing, contract, acceptance, revenue recognition, collection, delivery burden, gross-profit formation, sales cycle, repeat/renewal behavior, concentration, and scale constraints.

## 5. Normalized metrics

| Metric | Value | Period | Unit/currency | Definition/denominator | Actual/forecast | Source | Status |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Show formulas for calculations. Keep forecast data visually separate from actuals.

## 6. Team, capitalization, and financing

Record disclosed roles and relevant evidence, historical rounds, cap table, option pool, current raise, pre/post-money basis, use of proceeds, runway, and milestones. Mark missing fields rather than estimating them.

## 7. Material claims and evidence

| Claim | Status | Evidence/source | Counterevidence/conflict | Confidence | Required validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Confidence describes material support, not management integrity.

## 8. Conflicts and information gaps

Group by:

- internal conflict;
- unclear definition or period;
- material non-disclosure;
- stale or unreadable material;
- external verification required.

## 9. Next questions

| Priority | Question | Why it matters | Evidence requested | What it could change |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use P0 for a missing answer that prevents understanding the project, P1 for an item likely to affect quality or screening, and P2 for later diligence.

## 10. Handoff

In no more than 150 words state established facts, unsupported claims, the largest conflict or gap, and the most appropriate next skill. Close with: `This fact card organizes available information and is not an investment recommendation.`
