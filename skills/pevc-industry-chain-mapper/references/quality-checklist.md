# Quality checklist

## Structure

- Purpose, geography, time horizon, lifecycle judgment, demand state, and market definition are explicit.
- The main path is a real upstream-to-downstream flow.
- The chain can be read from scarce/enabling inputs through transformation to end demand.
- Investment judgment, valuation, risk, and exit are not stages.
- Cross-chain software, standards, or certification appear as support layers.
- Every edge has a label, direction, transmission mechanism, and source.
- Adjacent markets and exclusions are visible.

## Node coverage

- Every node has a plain-language definition.
- Inputs, transformation, outputs, immediate customers, and payer are specified.
- Company names are in details, not node titles.
- Companies have specific roles, evidence status, and company-role sources.
- Competing technology routes disclose maturity, trade-offs, and adoption signals.
- Node economics cover monetization, cost drivers, bargaining power, profit-pool logic, and transmission.
- Every node has 3–5 explained industry-specific metrics, an investment view, and 3–5 diligence questions.
- Every node has directly relevant sources for companies, technology routes, metrics, and edges.

## Evidence

- Facts, inference, and opinion are distinguishable.
- PoCs and partnerships are not described as production deployments.
- Representative customers are not described as verified adopters.
- Consequential size, share, deployment, capacity, pricing, and regulatory claims are triangulated when feasible; single-source limitations are visible.
- Market-size and concentration claims use consistent units, geography, period, and denominator.
- Source links resolve and support the nearby claim.
- The as-of date is displayed.

## Interaction and layout

- Initial view is readable and does not overlap.
- Clicking without dragging opens the correct node.
- Dragging does not accidentally open a detail.
- Trackpad and mouse navigation behave as labeled.
- Details can be closed with the button and Escape.
- Search finds title, company, and detail text.
- Opening a node highlights its directly connected nodes and edges.
- The node drawer explains incoming and outgoing transmission relationships.
- Support layers are visually distinct from chain stages.
- The page remains usable at narrow widths.
