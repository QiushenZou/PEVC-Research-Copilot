# Decision-ready memo structure

Use this as a modular structure, not a mandatory table of contents. The first page is a decision instrument; later sections prove or challenge it. Apply the depth in [stage-playbooks.md](stage-playbooks.md).

## Opening decision page

### 1. Decision requested

- Recommendation and exact authority requested
- Memo stage and information cutoff date
- Proposed investment, security, valuation, ownership, leverage or reserve, and timing
- Conditions, walk-away/repricing triggers, and unresolved decision-critical items
- Underwriting return range and principal downside when decisionable

### 2. Decision snapshot

| Item | Underwriting view | Evidence status | Main sensitivity or unresolved issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fund/strategy fit |  |  |  |
| Company and customer problem |  |  |  |
| Market access and timing |  |  |  |
| Product/competitive advantage |  |  |  |
| Team/execution |  |  |  |
| Economics/financial quality |  |  |  |
| Price, structure, and fund return |  |  |  |

### 3. Investment case and case against

State three to five independent thesis propositions. For each:

| Proposition | Supporting evidence | Counterevidence | Falsifier | Leading indicator | Decision consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then state the strongest coherent reason not to invest. Avoid a generic bear list.

## Core analysis modules

### 4. Company, product, and customer

- One-sentence description of what the company sells, to whom, why they buy, who signs, and who pays
- Product maturity, implementation, critical dependencies, and switching behavior
- Customer and revenue quality, including concentration, cohorts or repeat behavior where relevant
- Go-to-market motion, sales cycle, pricing, unit economics, and cash collection
- Verified win/loss reasons and evidence of moat or lack of moat

### 5. Market and value-chain position

- Precisely defined segment and boundary
- Demand drivers, adoption barriers, substitutes, and regulation
- Accessible market derived from customers, capacity, channels, or transactions—not only top-down TAM
- Value-chain position, profit pool, bargaining power, and supply/customer dependencies
- Competitor set, basis of competition, likely response, and market evolution

### 6. Team, organization, and governance

- Founder/management evidence relevant to the next stage of execution
- Learning velocity, recruiting ability, operating cadence, controls, and succession/key-person exposure
- Governance, incentives, related parties, references, and identified gaps
- Investor's realistic ability to influence outcomes

### 7. Historical performance and underwriting case

- Reconciled historical results and data-quality limitations
- Bridge from reported or management figures to normalized/underwriting figures
- Operating drivers and unit economics, not only growth percentages
- Management plan versus underwriting case
- Cash need, working capital, capex, runway, debt service, or covenant headroom as applicable
- Forecast accuracy and evidence supporting each material driver

### 8. Transaction and capitalization

- Sources and uses or round composition
- Pre/post capitalization and fully diluted ownership
- Existing securities, options, convertibles, debt, guarantees, preferences, anti-dilution, and senior claims
- Governance, information, pro rata, protective, transfer, and exit rights
- Future financing/refinancing needs and investor reserve obligations
- Deviations from the fund's normal terms and their decision impact

### 9. Valuation and returns

Use [valuation-and-returns.md](valuation-and-returns.md). Show:

- method and why it fits the business and stage;
- entry metric/date and benchmark adjustments;
- base, upside, and downside operating narratives;
- ownership/dilution or leverage/debt-paydown bridge;
- MOIC, IRR, timing, and fund contribution where relevant;
- sensitivity to the variables that can actually change the decision;
- price or terms at which the recommendation changes.

### 10. Risks, disconfirming evidence, and responses

| Loss mechanism | Current evidence/trigger | Transmission and impact | Mitigation/control | Residual exposure | Monitor | Decision response |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Include evidence against the thesis. Do not call ordinary execution work a mitigation unless it is owned, funded, and credible.

### 11. Diligence status and evidence gaps

| Issue/claim | Current evidence and confidence | Remaining test | Owner | Deadline | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Summarize completed, adverse, inconclusive, and not-started work. A list of workstreams is not a conclusion.

### 12. Approval conditions and post-investment plan

| Condition/action | Evidence of completion | Owner | Deadline | Failure/escalation consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Include conditions precedent, required documentation, first 100-day or next-milestone priorities, KPIs and thresholds, reporting cadence, reserve/refinancing choices, and exit or follow-on decision points.

## PE/control module

Add when relevant:

- QoE and normalized EBITDA bridge
- Cash conversion, working capital, maintenance/growth capex, tax, and leakage
- Sources and uses, debt tranches, amortization, cash sweep, covenant headroom, and liquidity downside
- Value-creation bridge split into organic growth, margin, M&A, deleveraging, and multiple change
- Management equity, rollover, governance, and succession
- Recovery and lender behavior in downside

## VC/minority module

Add when relevant:

- Product-market-fit evidence appropriate to stage
- Cohorts, retention/repeat behavior, engagement, gross-margin path, CAC/payback or other distribution economics
- Founder-market fit, velocity, talent density, and missing executives
- Milestones funded by this round, burn, runway, next-round dependency, and financing path
- Fully diluted ownership through future rounds, pro rata strategy, preference stack, and investor proceeds
- Exit value required for target fund return and plausible paths to reach it

## Update/addendum structure

Use the delta format in [stage-playbooks.md](stage-playbooks.md). Put the revised ask first, then changes to prior conditions, thesis, model, terms, risks, and action plan. Append the prior approval only for reference; do not obscure the delta with a full rewrite.

## Appendices

Use appendices for source register, claim ledger, detailed models, customer evidence, market calculations, legal/technical reports, capitalization detail, and meeting notes. Keep every decision-critical uncertainty visible in the main memo.
