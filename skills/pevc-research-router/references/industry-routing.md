# Industry routing

Use this reference to select metrics and verification paths, not to force a complete checklist.

| Business type | Decision variables | High-value evidence | Common category errors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software/SaaS | retention, expansion, gross-margin path, implementation burden, sales efficiency | cohort exports, contracts, usage, renewal and churn interviews | bookings or contract value called ARR; services excluded from delivery cost |
| Manufacturing/hard tech | maturity, yield, qualification, utilization, BOM, capex-to-capacity | production records, customer qualification, failure logs, purchase/acceptance records | sample or design win called mass production; nameplate capacity called output |
| Consumer/brand | sell-through, repeat, contribution, inventory, channel dependence | channel and SKU cohorts, returns, discounting, inventory aging, repeat purchase | sell-in called end demand; GMV called revenue |
| Marketplace/transaction | liquidity, take rate, subsidy dependence, local density, disintermediation | transaction logs, cohort contribution, supply/demand retention | gross transaction value called net revenue |
| Healthcare/life sciences | clinical/validation evidence, regulatory path, CMC, reimbursement, cash to milestone | trial registries, protocols, regulator records, manufacturing and payer evidence | early endpoint or preclinical signal called approval probability |
| Project/ToB/ToG | order quality, acceptance, gross margin, collections, repeatability | tender/contract, delivery, acceptance, invoice, receivable and cash chain | award called revenue; search miss called nonexistence |
| Infrastructure/asset-heavy | utilization, contracted revenue, construction/operating risk, financing, residual value | permits, EPC/operating records, contracts, debt terms, maintenance history | nameplate capacity called utilized capacity; EBITDA treated as free cash flow |

For unfamiliar sectors, first identify the physical or economic transaction, customer workflow, adoption gate, bottleneck, and source of value capture. Then choose the smallest set of sector metrics that can falsify the investment case.
