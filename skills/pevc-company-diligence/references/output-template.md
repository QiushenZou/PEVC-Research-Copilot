# Company diligence output template

Adapt the length to the decision stage. Do not fill sections with boilerplate when evidence is unavailable.

## 1. Decision snapshot

- Company, round or transaction, date, and scope
- Current view: proceed / proceed with conditions / pause / decline
- Confidence level and why
- Decisive supporting evidence and disconfirming evidence; include only what is material
- Unresolved gates and the next highest-value diligence actions

If the work is a focused verification sprint, replace the overall recommendation with `claim supported / partly supported / contradicted / inconclusive`, followed by the decision consequence.

## 2. Review scope and material register

| Material/source | Version/date | Type | Coverage reviewed | Unreadable/missing | Reliability note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

State explicitly whether external verification, interviews, system exports, contracts, and cash records were available.

## 3. How the company makes money

Explain the customer problem, buyer, offering, pricing, contract, revenue recognition, gross-profit formation, cash collection, and reinvestment needs as one connected mechanism.

## 4. Product and technology

Explain what the product does, where it sits, maturity, differentiation, dependencies, roadmap, and failure modes in language an investment committee can understand.

## 5. Customers and commercial evidence

Include concentration, commercial status, cohorts or retention where relevant, sales cycle, pipeline conversion, and the evidence supporting major customer claims.

Include a customer-status table for material accounts:

| Customer/account | Economic role | Highest verified status | Period/value | Evidence | Concentration/economics | Open test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Competition and moat

Name direct competitors, substitutes, internal build, and doing nothing. Explain why the company wins or loses and whether the advantage strengthens with scale.

## 7. Financial quality

Present reconciled historical trends and explain growth, gross margin, operating leverage, working capital, capex, cash conversion, runway, and financing requirements. Show the material revenue-to-cash and EBITDA-to-free-cash-flow bridges; add ARR, backlog/pipeline, gross-margin, or forecast-versus-actual bridges only when relevant.

## 8. Team and governance

Assess capability for the next stage, key-person dependencies, incentives, governance, and reporting reliability.

## 9. Transaction-specific view

- **PE/control:** normalized EBITDA, maintenance capex, cash conversion, debt capacity, working-capital/debt-like exposures, management rollover, operational levers, and downside covenant/refinancing case.
- **VC/minority:** stage-appropriate product-market proof, unit economics, burn/runway, milestone-to-cash plan, governance, dilution and preference stack, follow-on need, and fund-return exit requirements.

## 10. Key risks and red flags

For each material risk state the evidence, mechanism of loss, probability or uncertainty, impact, possible mitigant, affected deal term or condition, owner, resolution test, and monitoring indicator.

## 11. Decisive claims ledger

| Claim | Label | Source/locator | Period/scope | Supporting evidence | Counterevidence/conflict | Confidence | Decision consequence | Next test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 12. Prioritized diligence and request tracker

| Priority | Question/request | Why it matters | Evidence requested | Owner | Status/due date | What changes the view |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use `Not started`, `Requested`, `Received`, `In review`, `Complete`, `Blocked`, or `Red flag`. Do not mark an item complete merely because a document was received; complete means reviewed and reconciled to the affected claim.

## 13. Source notes and calculation appendix

List source links or file/page locators, interview date and respondent vantage point, public-search coverage, formulas, currency/unit conversions, and assumptions required to reproduce material calculations.
