# Evidence and red-flag protocol

## Evidence labels

Use one of these labels for material claims:

- **Verified fact:** directly supported by a reliable document, dataset, filing, contract, invoice, bank record, system export, or authoritative public source.
- **Management claim:** asserted by management but not independently verified.
- **Third-party estimate:** sourced externally with methodology and date disclosed.
- **Calculation:** reproducible transformation of stated inputs; show the formula and preserve input sources.
- **Inference:** reasoned conclusion from stated evidence; explain the chain of reasoning.
- **Investment view:** judgment about attractiveness, risk, or expected outcome.
- **Open question:** evidence is missing or contradictory and could change the decision.
- **Conflict:** two or more sources cannot be reconciled without additional information.

## Material register and claim ledger

Before analyzing, record each input's name, version/date, source, pages or sections reviewed, unreadable portions, and whether it is management-provided, system-generated, contractual, public, or third-party. A source is not strong merely because it is detailed.

For each decisive claim, capture:

| Claim | Label | Source and locator | Period/scope | Supporting evidence | Counterevidence/conflict | Confidence | Decision consequence | Next test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Confidence describes evidentiary support, not management integrity. Do not force a numeric confidence score. When sources conflict, preserve both versions, quantify the difference, seek the definition or source-of-record, and explain which conclusion depends on resolution.

## Customer and commercial claim ladder

Do not collapse these statuses:

1. Target account
2. Initial contact
3. Technical evaluation
4. Proof of concept or sample
5. Design win or preferred status
6. Signed order or contract
7. Shipment or service delivery
8. Customer acceptance
9. Revenue recognition
10. Cash collection
11. Repeat purchase or expansion
12. Durable production deployment

For important customers, state the highest status actually supported and the supporting evidence.

Do not call a customer relationship “verified” from a logo, press release, unsigned spreadsheet, or interview alone. Triangulate as far as access permits:

- Contract/order and amendments establish terms, not delivery.
- Invoice establishes billing, not acceptance or collection.
- Delivery/acceptance record or system usage supports actual deployment.
- Bank receipt or receivables subledger supports collection.
- Repeat order, renewal, expansion, or sustained usage supports durability.

Sample beyond management-selected references. When possible include top revenue, a new win, a renewal/expansion, a churned or lost account, and an account with unusual economics. Record whether the interviewee is a user, evaluator, buyer, procurement owner, or payer and the period they actually observed.

## External verification discipline

- Search company names, former names, subsidiaries, brands, and common abbreviations; state the databases, geographies, and dates covered.
- For public tenders/orders, distinguish procurement intent, tender notice, candidate announcement, award, signed contract, delivery/acceptance, revenue, and collection. Reconcile amounts and timing to disclosed revenue, but state coverage blind spots.
- For interviews, screen the respondent's role, recency, direct exposure, incentives, and conflicts. Start with a short calibration question set, then move from ranges to quantities and concrete examples. Cross-check material claims across independent vantage points when practical.
- Do not solicit confidential information, trade secrets, or material non-public information. Use lawful public sources and authorized materials.

## Common red flags

- Revenue growth without matching collections or with unusual receivable aging
- Recurring revenue that includes services, hardware, pilots, or cancellable commitments
- “Customers” that are partners, ecosystem members, distributors, or unpaid testers
- Gross margin excluding recurring delivery, warranty, cloud, support, or channel costs
- Repeated adjusted EBITDA add-backs or capitalized operating expenses
- Large end-period shipments, side letters, acceptance risk, returns, or rebates
- One customer, founder, supplier, certification, or technical component that can stop the business
- Pipeline presented without historical conversion and elapsed time by stage
- Market share calculated from an inconsistent market denominator
- Patents cited without freedom to operate, enforceability, product relevance, or ownership checks
- Growth plan requiring financing earlier than management's stated runway
- Prior forecasts repeatedly missing actuals without a reconciled bridge
- Reported growth concentrated in quarter-end activity, new entities, related parties, or customers with extended terms
- EBITDA growth that does not translate into operating cash because of receivables, inventory, capitalized costs, or maintenance capex
- Backlog or pipeline with stale stages, cancellation rights, weak deposits, duplicated opportunities, or no historical conversion evidence
- Customer references restricted to friendly accounts while churned, lost, concentrated, or low-margin customers are unavailable
- Different source systems produce materially different revenue, customer, headcount, or cash figures without a named source of record

## Prioritizing diligence questions

Rank each open question by:

- Probability the current assumption is wrong
- Magnitude of impact on valuation or recommendation
- Time and cost to verify
- Whether evidence is obtainable before the decision

The highest-priority question is not necessarily the largest unknown; it is the question with the greatest expected decision value.

Track each request through `Not started → Requested → Received → In review → Complete`, with `Blocked` and `Red flag` as explicit exception states. Include owner, due date, evidence requested, result, affected claim, and impact on valuation, structure, conditions, or recommendation. Escalate on the substance of what is missing or contradictory; response delay alone is only a signal.
