## Fund-fit table

| Criterion | Fund requirement | Project evidence | Status | Source | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use only criteria supplied by the fund or user. Label status `Pass`, `Fail`, `Unclear`, or `Not applicable`.

## Project mechanism

Explain the customer problem, product, buyer/payer, purchasing event, revenue and gross-profit mechanism, cash collection, and principal scaling constraint. Highlight whether the record supports prototype, pilot, signed order, delivery, acceptance, revenue, collection, or repeat demand.

## Initial investment case and case against

Use no more propositions than the evidence supports:

| Proposition | Supporting evidence | Counterevidence | Falsifier | Decision implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then state the strongest coherent reason not to proceed.

## Plausible return path

### PE/control

Use the available earnings definition, cash conversion, likely leverage, required equity, operational levers, downside liquidity, and exit range. Mark returns as feasibility estimates until terms and normalized earnings tie.

### VC/minority

Use round size, ownership, future capital and dilution, reserve need, milestone path, and exit value required for a fund-relevant outcome. Do not confuse company success with a meaningful fund return.

## Fatal questions and minimum tests

| Priority | Fatal question | Current assumption | Cheapest credible test | Evidence required | Time/cost | Stop/reprice condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Questions should be specific enough to fail. Avoid generic requests such as “validate the market” or “understand competition.”
